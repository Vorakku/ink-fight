# Ant armies and evolving bases

Raise the shared living creature limit to 1,000. Each ant side builds one base and upgrades it at the existing 10/20/40/80/160 worker milestones, instead of placing additional hills. Five visual tiers add palisades, bones, a dragon skull, and T-Rex claw trophies. Base size and health grow with its tier. The fourth completed tier earns the existing sheltered queen.

Keep actual living ants underground, counting toward the global cap. Initial construction assembles twenty residents. Food delivered by collectors adds pending recruits; production retries at capacity. Under attack, deploy at most twenty residents, spaced a quarter second apart. Surviving attackers return when safe. Tanks remain near home to guard it.

Unlock tanks at tier two, slashers at tier three, and flying transports at tier four. Birth rolls determine the caste; ordinary workers remain available for construction and hauling. Tanks have armor and a timed provoke that redirects nearby enemies. Slashers prioritize fighting and inflict bleeding. Flyers carry an allied deployed worker or slasher toward an enemy and drop them safely near combat. Release passengers on death, conversion, fear, stun, or invalid alliances.

Use existing Canvas sprites, bleeding, targeting, housing, and flight mechanics. Add focused executable checks, but do not run tests or other verification without the owner's explicit approval.
