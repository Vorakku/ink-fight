# Scorch's evolution and cave lair

The owner approved the proposed five forms, personal lair, resting, rainbow boss
and offspring. Any surviving dragon may become a boss; there is no per-team cap.

| Meals | Form | New power |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Green Young Dragon | Existing fire breath |
| 3 | Red Fire Drake | Explosive fireballs |
| 8 | Blue Storm Dragon | Chain lightning |
| 15 | Violet Ancient Dragon | Flight and a committed dive-bomb |
| 25 | Rainbow Prismatic Boss | Charged rainbow breath and egg laying |

Earlier powers remain available. Meals retain full healing and uncapped health
and damage gains, but do not increase adult body size. Completed adult forms add
8% of the original body size per stage (up to 32% at rainbow).
After reaching a threshold, return to a personal cave during a quiet interval,
curl into a luminous shell for two seconds and emerge one stage higher. Damage,
nearby enemies and incapacitation interrupt the ritual without removing earned
meals or completed stages. Cave decoration follows its owner's completed stage.

From the first threshold, build a cave on local dry ground within 120px, with
clearance from other buildings. Construction takes three seconds at its entrance.
No suitable local ground means wait. One personal cave; destroyed caves can be
rebuilt. Caves are ordinary targetable buildings. Attacking a cave wakes its
living owner, which defends it. An owner dying does not delete an intact cave.

With no nearby prey, dragons return home and sleep curled over treasure. Rest
heals 3% of maximum health per second; it does not grant invulnerability. Damage
or a nearby enemy wakes the dragon. Snoring smoke and sleep bubbles tell the story.

Bosses earn one egg per four meals beyond 25. Lay at home, at most two incubating
eggs per cave. Incubation is 20 seconds. Eggs are attackable, and cave destruction
destroys its eggs. A full page or obstructed exit delays hatching, preserving the
egg. Children inherit the exact side, start at zero meals and tier zero, and use
45% adult body size, health and damage. They grow gradually to adulthood over 60
seconds; each meal adds 15% juvenile progress and still fully heals them. Health
capacity and power follow their physical maturity. Natural growth increases
current health only by the added capacity, preserving existing wounds. Juveniles
must mature fully before evolving; earlier meals still count toward thresholds.
Once adult, only evolution increases their size. Children build their own caves.

Use cached original Canvas art for forms, folded/flapping wings, curled sleepers,
caves and eggs. Rainbow colours change slowly using cached variants. No labels,
screen flashes, new dependencies, or tests/builds/lint/browser checks without the
owner's explicit testing approval.
