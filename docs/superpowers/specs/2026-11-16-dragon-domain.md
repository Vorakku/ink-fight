# Dragon lightning, golden blast, and fire domain

Replace the lightning dragon's breath and chain visuals with irregular, branching bolts with bright cores, forks, and discharge sparks. Add electrical arcs to evolved dragon auras. The golden form uses a single golden energy blast for basic attacks, without the combined fire/chill effects; retain its chain-lightning skill, ice dive, and lightning sword.

Only mature golden Super Dragons can activate one emergency domain per life, below 50% maximum health with exposed enemies nearby. Fire, Ice and Lightning forms cannot activate it or spend its use. They rise for 0.8 seconds, roar for 0.9 seconds, then descend over 0.4 seconds. At the roar's end, seal a 340-pixel-radius fire arena around exposed enemies, including flyers. Allies and sheltered residents remain safe. The ring holds for three seconds, then shrinks over twelve seconds. The caster lands and can fight during the countdown; enemies focus it. Killing or converting the caster immediately ends the domain. Interrupting the opening cast also cancels it.

Keep trapped creatures and the caster inside the shrinking wall, including after knockback, throws, dashes, or flight. Reveal hidden captives and release their movement anchors. New enemies entering the arena become trapped too. The completed collapse kills surviving captives, with normal kill credit and Phoenix rebirth behavior. Buildings are outside the captive set. Clear removes all domain state.

Use the existing Canvas renderer and simulation state. Leave runnable regression checks, but do not run any checks, builds, previews, or verification without the owner's explicit permission.
